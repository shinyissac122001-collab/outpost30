🚀 Outpost 30

Can you keep your crew safe for 30 days?

You are the Commander of a new space outpost. Every day you get power from the Sun. Split it between air, food, shields and science. No choice is free, just like for real NASA mission planners!

🌕 Moon Base

Near the Moon's south pole
  • Lots of sunlight, but dark spells (you can see them coming)
  • No air at all, so radiation is high
  • Earth is only 1.3 light-seconds away

🔴 Mars Base

In Jezero Crater, Mars
  • Less sunlight and surprise dust storms
  • Thin air gives a little radiation protection
  • Messages to Earth take minutes
How to play: 1) Read the news card and pick what to do. 2) Use + and − to share out today's ⚡ power. 3) Press End Day. Leftover power is saved in the battery. Lose if air or food runs out, or the crew's radiation dose goes over the limit. Every event is based on real NASA science. Tap 📚 to see where it comes from.
